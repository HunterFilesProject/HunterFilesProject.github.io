About the Hunter Files Project

The Hunter Files Project is a contextual database for hunter files — a searchable collection of historical and documentary records, organized into volumes and, where a volume is large enough to need it, further split into datasets. Each document page includes its available context, referenced people, keywords, current status, and links to related documents.

Use the search bar to find documents by title, person, date, location, status, or keyword, or browse by volume and dataset using the sidebar. The Dashboard, Timeline, Keywords, and Changelog pages offer other ways into the same underlying files.
